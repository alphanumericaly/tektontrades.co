Some materials walk in the door with a story already attached.

Job 1010 came with a western red cedar slab that had been sitting outside, slowly returning to the earth. Sentimental to the homeowner, but neglected. We milled it down, flattened it, and turned it into the live edge butcher block counter that anchors the entire built-in. The natural edge was preserved and the character of the wood — knots, grain variation, the history of it — became the centerpiece of the room.

Above the counter, walnut floating shelves carry the warmth of the cedar upward. Walnut and cedar don't always get paired together but in this space, against this wall, they work.

This is what custom means. Not picking from a catalog, but taking what someone already has and making it into something they'll keep forever.
