Two rooms, one vision, zero compromise.

Job 1016 was a dual installation — a laundry room and a pantry built out simultaneously, each matching the other in style and finish. Butcher block counters run over the base cabinets in both rooms, with the laundry room wrapping over the washer and dryer for a clean, seamless utility space.

The large storage cabinets give both rooms serious function, but the standout piece is the custom cherry laundry rack — built from solid cherry, designed to fold out when needed and tuck away when not. Cherry was chosen for its warmth and the way it ages. Twenty years from now that rack will be more beautiful than it is today.

Matching two rooms requires discipline. Every dimension, every reveal, every finish decision has to be made twice and executed identically. That kind of consistency doesn't happen by accident.
