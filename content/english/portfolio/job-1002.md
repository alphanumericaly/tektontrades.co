Walnut has a way of making an awkward space look like it was planned that way all along.

Job 1002 was a living room with an odd wall — too big to leave empty, too irregular to fill with store bought furniture. A custom walnut bookshelf was the answer. Floor to ceiling, wall to wall, built specifically for that room and no other.

The detail that made this one special — built-in power, routed cleanly through the unit and hidden from view, specifically to power a Christmas village display the homeowner sets up every year. Function and sentiment in the same piece.

This was an early job for Tekton and it set the tone for everything that followed. The client came back. That's the whole point.
