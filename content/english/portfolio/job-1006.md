For a homeowner in a boating community, loading and unloading the boat is a near daily chore. 

The client had a number of design constraints to meet for the perfect stairs. He needed them to be just tall enough to be able to back the boat directly over the top step, easy to move, weather proof, and to look good while doing it to name a few. While they aren't much to look at, they're the perfect solution for him.

Built with mortise and tenon joinery, they only fasteners in this project are screws holding the stair treads in place. The stairs were finished with a marine grade spar varnish, and will remain like new for many boating seasons to come.
