A closet is only as useful as what's built inside it.

Job 1054 was a closet build-out: two closets, each fitted with storage cabinets and open shelving sized for what actually goes in them. Not a wire rack kit. Cabinets and shelves built for these two openings and nothing else.

Each side has a job. The left closet holds household cleaning supplies and the miscellaneous things every house collects. The right closet holds the home's networking equipment, tucked out of the way where it belongs.

The detail that makes it custom is the angled shelf, built specifically for rolls of wrapping paper. Nobody sells that at the big box store. When storage is designed around the things you own, everything ends up with a place to live.
