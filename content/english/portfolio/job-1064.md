The laundry room is the hardest working room in the house. It should be built like it.

Job 1064 was a laundry room build-out: a sink, cabinets, and one cabinet with a very specific tenant. It's not the first laundry room Tekton has built out. [Job 1016](/portfolio/job-1016/) was a laundry room and pantry.

The standout is the doggy crate cabinet. Instead of a wire crate parked in the corner, the crate is built into the cabinetry as a piece of the room. The dog gets a spot of their own and the room keeps its clean lines.

That's what custom is for. A laundry room planned around everyone who uses it, four legged ones included.
