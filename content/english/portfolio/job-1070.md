Zero fasteners. The wood holds itself together.

Job 1070 is a stand-alone dresser built from thermo red oak — red oak that has been heat treated, which darkens the color and makes the wood more stable. It's furniture, not a built-in, so it has to stand on its own. Every joint in it is mechanical: wood locked into wood, with no fasteners doing the work.

The drawers are half-blind rabbeted dovetails, chosen to highlight the joint. From the front you see a clean drawer face. Pull the drawer open and the dovetails are right there on the side.

The drawers ride on under-mount slides, hidden beneath the drawer box so the hardware never competes with the joinery.

It sits on adjustable leveling feet with soft rubber pads, so it stands level on a floor that isn't and never marks it.
