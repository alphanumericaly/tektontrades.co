Some projects are just furniture. This one is a memory.

Job 1035 was a nursery built-in designed around a baby girl who hadn't arrived yet. The crib anchors the center, flanked by custom built-in storage and shelving that will grow with her long after the crib is gone. Built to last not just years, but phases of a childhood.

There's something different about building for a space like this. Every detail gets a little more attention than usual.
