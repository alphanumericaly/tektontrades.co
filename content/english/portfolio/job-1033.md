Not every job is about building something new. Sometimes the best work is the work nobody notices.

Job 1033 was a kitchen island that was simply too big for the space. The homeowner wanted more room to move, but didn't want to lose the look of their existing kitchen. The solution — rebuild a smaller version of the same cabinets, then put the original face frame and doors back on them at the new dimensions.

The result is seamless. The island matches the surrounding cabinetry perfectly because the parts you see are the originals. Same face frame, same doors. You'd never know it was touched, which is exactly the point.

This is the kind of problem solving that doesn't show up in a photo but means everything to the client living in that kitchen every day.
