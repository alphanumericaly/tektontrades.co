Not every job is about building something new. Sometimes the best work is the work nobody notices.

Job 1033 was a kitchen island that was simply too big for the space. The homeowner wanted more room to move, but didn't want to lose the look of their existing kitchen. The solution — reduce the island using all of the original frames, doors, and drawers, rebuilt and reassembled to the new dimensions.

The result is seamless. The island matches the surrounding cabinetry perfectly because it is the surrounding cabinetry. Same doors, same hardware, same finish. You'd never know it was touched, which is exactly the point.

This is the kind of problem solving that doesn't show up in a photo but means everything to the client living in that kitchen every day.
