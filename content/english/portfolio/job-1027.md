White oak has a way of making everything look intentional.

Job 1027 was a French pantry with a refrigerator already in place — the shelving had to be built around it, custom fitted to the space with no room for error. White oak was the material of choice and it was the right call. The grain, the color, the way it takes a finish — it elevated what could have been a purely functional storage solution into something worth showing off.

Every shelf was sized and positioned specifically for how this client uses their pantry. That's the difference between custom and off the shelf.
