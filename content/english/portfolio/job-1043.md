The best compliment a client can give is calling you back.

Job 1043 was custom walnut trim for a long-time client. They already had me build the walnut bookshelf on [Job 1002](/portfolio/job-1002/), one of the earliest Tekton jobs. This time the walnut went into the trim.

Trim is usually painted and forgotten. In walnut, it becomes something you notice. Anywhere a client wants to showcase wood, I get happy, and walnut is as good as it gets.

When someone trusts you with their home more than once, the relationship is the real project.
