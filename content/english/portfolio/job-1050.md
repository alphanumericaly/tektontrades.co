Most TVs get hung on a wall. This one got a wall built for it.

Job 1050 is a TV wall built-in: cabinets with inset doors, and bookshelves built as torsion boxes. It's the most recent Tekton build, and it uses two of the details I reach for whenever a piece is meant to be looked at every single day.

The cabinet doors are inset, meaning they sit flush inside the face frame instead of on top of it. It takes tighter tolerances to pull off, and it's the mark of fine cabinetry.

The bookshelves are torsion boxes. Built like an airplane wing, with two flat surfaces over a rigid internal grid, they stay stiff and flat under a full load of books without needing support in the middle of the span.
