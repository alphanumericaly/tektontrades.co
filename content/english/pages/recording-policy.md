## Why We Record

So we don't miss anything. Every detail you share about your project goes directly into building your estimate.

## Your Privacy

Everything stays local — recordings are processed on our equipment, not uploaded to the cloud or shared with anyone.

## What We Keep

- **Audio recording** — deleted when your job is complete
- **Full transcription** — deleted when your job is complete
- **Summary notes** — kept as part of our project records

## Consent

Florida law requires your informed consent to record, and you can revoke it at any time. Just let us know.
